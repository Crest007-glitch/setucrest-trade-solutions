SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS

Clear responsibilities before a transaction.

SetuCrest provides sourcing research and coordination as agreed. Supplier identification is not a guarantee of performance. The buyer remains responsible for technical approval, due diligence, contracts, payments, product compliance, import requirements and final transaction decisions. Logistics, inspection and certification can only be treated as included when separately confirmed in writing.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before starting an India sourcing enquiry.

Can I enquire before knowing the exact Indian supplier?

Yes. Begin with the product, quantity, destination and the specifications you already know.

Can you support more than one product?

Yes, but related products with a clear priority are easier to assess than a very broad unsorted list.

Do you guarantee the supplier?

No. We can coordinate research and information, while formal due diligence and transaction approval remain the buyer’s responsibility.
