BEFORE REQUESTING A QUOTE

Share enough information to avoid misleading prices.

  • Exact unit quantity for each product or variant
  • Destination country, postal code or Indian city
  • Required material, dimensions and quality level
  • Branding, packaging and labeling expectations
  • Acceptable substitutes or stock-product flexibility
  • Whether this is a trial or recurring requirement

LIMITATIONS

Not every small order is economical.

Some factories will not accept lower volumes, and international delivery can make a technically available product commercially unsuitable. SetuCrest will not promise a target price, MOQ or shipment outcome before suppliers and relevant service providers confirm the requirement.

FAQ

Small-quantity sourcing questions.

What counts as a small quantity?

It depends on the product, production method and supplier. Ten customized units may be small for one category, while several hundred can still be small for another.

Will small quantities cost more per unit?

Often yes, because setup, packaging and handling are distributed across fewer units.

Can multiple products be combined?

Sometimes, particularly through wholesalers, but consolidation and delivery responsibilities must be separately confirmed.
