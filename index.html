FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about sourcing from India and finding buyers.

Can you help overseas buyers source products from India?

Yes. We support international buyers with supplier discovery, requirement sharing, quotation coordination, sample follow-up and commercial communication in India.

Do you handle small and medium-quantity sourcing?

Yes. Our service is designed for practical small and medium requirements as well as businesses that are not ready for full-container import-export volumes.

Can you source products from Delhi and the NCR region?

Yes. Our Delhi-NCR sourcing desk assists businesses outside the region with local supplier options, specifications, quotations and sample coordination.

How do you help Indian manufacturers find buyers?

We research suitable markets, importers, distributors, institutions and decision-makers, then support focused outreach and follow-up around the manufacturer's offering.

Does SetuCrest act as the importer or exporter?

SetuCrest provides independent sourcing and business-development support. Contracting, compliance, payment, logistics and shipment responsibilities are agreed by the parties for each transaction.

How does payment work for small and medium orders?

For SetuCrest-managed individual, small and medium orders, 100% payment is required after the client approves the digital product option and before SetuCrest purchases the goods or arranges dispatch. This arrangement is agreed during the initial call and confirmed in the quotation.

How are sourcing and handling costs calculated?

Small, personal and medium orders are quoted as the product cost plus actual shipping and an approximately 8% handling margin. If supplier, packaging or shipping costs change, the revised amount is confirmed before purchase.

Do you send physical samples before payment?

The initial review is conducted online using product information, photos, videos and digital samples. Physical samples can be arranged separately when required, with their cost and delivery charges confirmed before dispatch.

How are small and large shipments handled?

Smaller orders may be packed and dispatched through an authorised international courier. Larger consignments may be coordinated through a third-party exporter or logistics partner, with responsibilities stated in the quotation.
