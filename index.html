FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about sourcing from India and finding buyers.

Can you help overseas buyers source products from India?

Yes. We support international buyers with supplier discovery, requirement sharing, quotation coordination, sample follow-up and commercial communication in India.

Do you handle small and medium-quantity sourcing?

Yes. Our service is designed for practical small and medium requirements as well as businesses that are not ready for full-container import-export volumes.

Can you source products from Delhi and the NCR region?

Yes. Our Delhi-NCR sourcing desk assists businesses outside the region with local supplier options, specifications, quotations and sample coordination.

How do you help Indian manufacturers find buyers?

We research suitable markets, importers, distributors, institutions and decision-makers, then support focused outreach and follow-up around the manufacturer's offering.

Does SetuCrest act as the importer or exporter?

SetuCrest provides independent sourcing and business-development support. Contracting, compliance, payment, logistics and shipment responsibilities are agreed by the parties for each transaction.
