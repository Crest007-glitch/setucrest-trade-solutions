THE PROBLEM

A long supplier list is not the same as a usable shortlist.

Directories can surface many companies, but the buyer still needs to know who handles the product, quantity, specification and destination. Our role is to make that first commercial comparison more structured.

Manufacturers

Useful where capability, repeat supply, specification control or customization matters.

Wholesalers

Often practical for mixed requirements, lower quantities and readily available products.

Specialist suppliers

Relevant for technical, regional or product-specific requirements that need focused knowledge.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

Supplier discovery is not supplier certification.

Unless a documented verification scope is separately agreed, SetuCrest does not label a supplier “verified.” Public information, supplier statements and preliminary communication must be followed by appropriate buyer-led legal, technical, financial and quality checks before payment or contracting.

FAQ

Supplier-search questions.

Can you find suppliers in Delhi-NCR?

Yes. Delhi-NCR is an important sourcing base, and the search may also cover other Indian clusters when they better fit the product.

Will every supplier provide a quotation?

No. Response depends on product fit, quantity, clarity, capacity and the supplier’s commercial interest.

Can you compare manufacturers and wholesalers?

Yes, when both routes are relevant. The practical choice may differ based on quantity, customization and lead time.
