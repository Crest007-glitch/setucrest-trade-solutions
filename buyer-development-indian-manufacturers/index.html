BEFORE OUTREACH

A product catalogue alone is rarely a market-development strategy.

Buyer development begins by defining which organization has a reason to buy, how the product fits its portfolio or operations, and what evidence supports the proposition.

Market focus

Prioritise countries, regions or domestic segments using product fit and commercial practicality.

Buyer definition

Distinguish distributors, importers, institutions, integrators and end buyers before building lists.

Credible proposition

Connect product capability, documentation and commercial terms to a buyer-relevant reason to respond.

From manufacturer readiness to measured outreach.

MANUFACTURER INPUTS

Information needed before buyer research.

  • Product catalogue and priority products
  • Target countries, regions or buyer segments
  • Capacity, MOQ, lead time and commercial terms
  • Certifications and test documents where relevant
  • Existing markets and excluded territories
  • Clear differentiators and customization capability

REALISTIC LIMITATIONS

Research and outreach cannot guarantee an order.

SetuCrest does not promise buyer responses, meetings, distributorships or sales. Results depend on product-market fit, price, documentation, timing, buyer demand and the manufacturer’s ability to respond. Any representation, exclusivity, commission or transaction role must be agreed separately in writing.
